## Today

This site has no newsletter sign-up and I don't run a marketing email list from it. If you email me, I reply to that conversation. That isn't marketing, and you won't be added to any list.

My writing is published on [Substack](https://substack.com/@camtoando). If you subscribe there, Substack handles the sign-up, the sending and the unsubscribe link under its own terms.

If you buy a product, Gumroad may offer you the choice to receive updates from me. That choice is yours and every such email has an unsubscribe link.

## If this changes

Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) and the GDPR both apply to marketing email. If I start a list, these rules apply from day one:

1. **Opt-in only.** An unticked box or a dedicated sign-up. Never bundled with a purchase or with these terms.
2. **Say what you're signing up for** and who is sending it, at the moment you sign up.
3. **Confirm by email** (double opt-in) so nobody can sign up someone else.
4. **Every message identifies me**, includes a contact address, and has a working unsubscribe link.
5. **Unsubscribes are honoured within 10 business days** (in practice, straight away).
6. **Proof of consent is kept:** who, when, how, and the wording they saw.
7. **No bought or scraped lists.** Ever.

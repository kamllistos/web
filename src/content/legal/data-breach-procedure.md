## What counts as a breach

Any loss of, unauthorised access to, or unauthorised disclosure of personal information. For this site that realistically means: my mailbox is compromised, a provider I use is breached, or a client's information I hold is exposed.

## The procedure

1. **Contain.** Stop it getting worse: lock the account, rotate passwords and keys, revoke access, take the affected thing offline if needed.
2. **Assess.** What information, whose, how many people, how sensitive, and is there a real risk of significant harm (identity theft, financial loss, humiliation)?
3. **Notify.** If there's a real risk of significant harm:
   - **The people affected**, directly and as soon as feasible, in plain language: what happened, what information, what I've done, what they should do, how to reach me.
   - **The regulator:** the Alberta Information and Privacy Commissioner, and the federal Privacy Commissioner where federal law applies, without unreasonable delay. If people in the EU or UK are affected, their authority within 72 hours of my becoming aware.
   - **Clients**, without delay, if the information belongs to their business, so they can meet their own duties.
4. **Record.** Every breach is written down, including ones too small to notify: date, what happened, what was affected, the decision and the reasons. Records are kept for at least 24 months.
5. **Fix and learn.** Find the root cause, fix it, and write a blameless review. Public-facing fixes show on the [changelog](/changelog/).

## If a provider is breached

I follow their notices, work out whether anyone I deal with is affected, and run the same steps.

## Report something

If you think information connected to this site has been exposed, email [official@camtoan.me](mailto:official@camtoan.me). For vulnerabilities, see the [disclosure policy](/security).

## If this changes

If the site ever stores personal data itself, add before launch: alerts that would actually detect a breach, a list of who to contact kept outside the affected systems, and a practice run of this procedure.

## The rule

Keep information only as long as there's a reason to, then delete it.

## Schedule

| Information | Kept for | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theme choice in your browser | Until you clear it | It's on your device, under your control |
| Host request data (IP address, pages requested) | Cloudflare's own schedule | I don't store a copy |
| Email enquiries that don't lead to work | Up to 12 months | To follow up, then no reason to keep it |
| Client emails, reports and working files | The length of the engagement plus the period in our written agreement | To do the work and answer later questions |
| Access to a client's systems | Removed when the engagement ends | No reason to keep access |
| Invoices and tax records | 6 years after the end of the tax year | Required by Canadian tax law |
| Purchase records | Held by Gumroad under its policy | Gumroad runs the checkout |
| Security incident records | At least 24 months | Required by Canadian privacy law |

## How deletion happens

Email is deleted from the mailbox and its trash. Files are deleted from where they're stored and from backups as those backups expire.

## If this changes

Every new kind of data gets a row here **before** it's collected. If the site ever has a database, retention is enforced by an automatic job rather than by memory, and backups follow the same limits.

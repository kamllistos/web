## How to ask

Email [official@camtoan.me](mailto:official@camtoan.me) from the address the request is about, and say what you want deleted. You don't need to give a reason.

## What happens

1. **I confirm it's you**, usually by replying to the address in question. I won't ask for more identification than the request needs.
2. **I delete** what I hold: your emails and any notes or files tied to you.
3. **I ask providers to delete** where it's in their systems (for example, your customer record at Gumroad).
4. **I confirm in writing** when it's done, within 30 days of your request.

## What I can't delete

- **Records the law makes me keep**, such as invoices for tax purposes. These are kept for the required period and then deleted. See [Data retention](/legal/data-retention/).
- **Host request logs** at Cloudflare, which I don't control and which expire on their own schedule.
- **Things you published yourself** on other platforms.

If I can't delete something, I'll tell you what and why.

## Other requests

The same email address works for asking what I hold about you, getting a copy, or correcting it.

## If this changes

If accounts are ever added, deletion must be self-service: a "delete my account" button in settings that removes the account, its data and its backups within a stated period, without needing to email anyone.

## Today: no cookies, no banner

kamllistos.com sets **no cookies**. That's why you don't see a cookie banner: there's nothing to ask your consent for.

The only thing saved in your browser is one item in local storage:

| Name | Purpose | Set when | Lifetime | Shared with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `theme` | Remembers whether you picked light or dark mode | You click Light or Dark | Until you click Auto or clear your browser data | Nobody. It stays on your device. |

This is strictly needed to do what you asked, so it doesn't need consent. Click **Auto** in the top bar to remove it.

Cloudflare, the host, may set its own security cookies in rare cases (for example during an attack) to tell people and bots apart. Those are security cookies, not tracking.

## If this changes

If a non-essential cookie or tracker is ever added (analytics, embedded video, ads, a chat widget), these rules apply first:

1. **Ask before setting anything.** Nothing non-essential loads until you say yes.
2. **Rejecting is as easy as accepting.** Same size buttons, one click each, no pre-ticked boxes.
3. **Choices by category** (analytics, marketing), not all-or-nothing.
4. **You can change your mind** at any time from a link in the footer.
5. **Every cookie is listed** in the table above with its purpose, lifetime and who receives it.
6. **Proof of consent is kept** (what was agreed to, when, which version of the notice).

The site's Content-Security-Policy blocks third-party scripts by default, so a tracker can't be added by accident.

## The promise

I don't sell personal information. I don't share it for advertising. It only goes to the providers that run the site, and only as far as each one needs.

## Who sees what

| Provider | Role | What it sees | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) | Hosting, DNS, security | IP address, browser type, pages requested | Worldwide network (US company) |
| [Proton Mail](https://proton.me/legal/privacy) | Email | Emails you send me | Switzerland |
| [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/privacy) | Product checkout | Name, email, payment and tax details, if you buy | United States |

## Who doesn't

- **No analytics, advertising or social media trackers.** None are installed, and the site's security policy blocks third-party scripts.
- **Fonts, scripts and images are served from this site**, so your browser doesn't contact anyone else to load a page.
- **GitHub** builds the site and hosts the status page. It never receives visitor data from kamllistos.com.

## When I'd disclose without asking

Only if the law requires it (a valid court order, for example), or to report a crime or protect someone's safety. I'd disclose the minimum and, where I'm allowed to, tell you.

## If this changes

A new provider is added to this table **before** it goes live. The contract side is on [Data processing agreements](/legal/data-processing-agreements/).

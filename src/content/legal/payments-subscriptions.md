## Today

**This site takes no payments itself.** There's no checkout here and no card details ever touch it.

**Products** are sold through [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com). Gumroad runs the checkout, takes the payment, handles sales tax and sends the receipt. Prices are shown on each product's page before you pay. I see your name, email and what you bought, never your card number. For a refund, email [official@camtoan.me](mailto:official@camtoan.me) and I'll handle it through Gumroad.

**Services** are quoted and agreed in writing before any work starts. Prices on the [Services](/services/) page are in Canadian dollars. The quote states the price, what's included and how to pay.

**The monthly retainer** is the only recurring charge I offer. Its price, what's included and how to end it are set out in the written agreement.

## If this changes

If subscriptions or payments are ever added to this site, these rules apply from day one:

1. **Never handle card data directly.** Use a payment provider's hosted checkout so card numbers never reach this site.
2. **Show the full price before payment:** amount, currency, taxes, billing period, and when it renews.
3. **No surprise renewals.** Free trials say when they end and what they'll cost. Annual plans get a reminder before renewing.
4. **Cancelling is as easy as signing up.** Online, in a couple of clicks, with no phone call or retention maze.
5. **Clear refund terms**, written before the first sale.
6. **Receipts for every charge**, and a notice before any price change.
7. **Trust the provider, not the browser.** Access is granted from the payment provider's verified webhook, never from a "success" page.
8. **Failed payments** get a notice and a grace period before access is removed.
